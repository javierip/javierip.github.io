## Open-Source Projects

### Video understanding with small models

- [pico-JEPA v2](https://github.com/BHI-Research/pico-jepa-v2){:target="_blank"}: an ensemble of tiny JEPA models that can beat one larger model
- [pico-JEPA](https://github.com/BHI-Research/pico-JEPA){:target="_blank"}: ultra-light JEPA models combined by voting
- [nano-JEPA](https://github.com/BHI-Research/nano-jepa){:target="_blank"}: a video JEPA you can train on a personal computer
- [nano-datasets](https://github.com/BHI-Research/nano-datasets){:target="_blank"}: builds small custom subsets of large video datasets

### Video summarization

- [Flash Video Summarization](https://github.com/javierip/flash-video-summarization){:target="_blank"}: summarization that runs faster than real time
- Open Summarization Toolbox: evaluation tools in [Python](https://github.com/BHI-Research/ost-python){:target="_blank"} and [C++](https://github.com/BHI-Research/ost){:target="_blank"}
- [OST datasets](https://github.com/BHI-Research/ost-datasets){:target="_blank"}: datasets widely used in video summarization research

### Optimization and parallel computing education

- [PSO-JAX](https://github.com/BHI-Research/PSO-JAX){:target="_blank"}: accelerated Particle Swarm Optimization built on JAX
- [Edu-PSO](https://github.com/BHI-Research/Edu-PSO){:target="_blank"}: PSO for teaching parallel processing, in Python, C++, OpenMP, CUDA, and JavaScript
- [Parallel Processing Teaching Toolkit](https://github.com/javierip/parallel-processing-teaching-toolkit){:target="_blank"}: examples and course material
