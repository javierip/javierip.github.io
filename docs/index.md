I am **Director of Machine Learning** at [Teladoc Health](https://www.teladochealth.com/){:target="_blank"}, where I lead machine learning teams that turn research into reliable, compliant production systems with measurable business and clinical impact.

I am also a **Professor** at [Universidad Tecnológica Nacional, Facultad Regional Bahía Blanca](https://www.frbb.utn.edu.ar/){:target="_blank"} and lead the [BHI-Research](https://bhi-research.github.io/){:target="_blank"} group. Our research focuses on **video understanding, self-supervised learning, and small, efficient models** that run on hardware people already own. I am based in Bahía Blanca, Argentina.
