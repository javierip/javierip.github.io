# Leadership

I work where **regulated, production-grade AI** meets **applied research**. My focus is building AI teams and systems that add lasting business value instead of technical debt.

## How I Lead

### 1. Business Outcomes and Reliable Production

At **[Teladoc Health](https://www.teladochealth.com/){:target="_blank"}**, I lead machine learning teams working in a healthcare setting with high standards for trust, privacy, and compliance. In practice that means:

* **Outcomes first:** R&D ideas earn engineering time by showing clear product, clinical, or cost impact.
* **Reliable production:** End-to-end MLOps with monitoring, drift detection, privacy safeguards, and audit trails.
* **Cross-functional alignment:** Working closely with Product, Clinical, Legal, and Infrastructure leaders so the AI roadmap supports company goals.

### 2. Cost-Aware AI Architecture

Inference and training costs can quietly erode the value of an AI product. I design systems with cost in mind from the start:

* **Right-sized models:** Where the task allows, specialized self-supervised models (such as **JEPA** variants) can match or beat large general-purpose models at a fraction of the compute cost. Our [pico-JEPA v2](https://bhi-research.github.io/pico-jepa-v2/){:target="_blank"} work is one example.
* **Everyday hardware:** Memory-efficient training and fast data pipelines that run on consumer GPUs with 6–8 GB of memory, or on edge devices.

### 3. Connecting Research, Teaching, and Industry

As a Professor at **UTN FRBB** and leader of the **[BHI-Research](https://bhi-research.github.io/){:target="_blank"}** group, I keep a direct line between new research and real-world engineering:

* **Early evaluation of new ideas:** We test emerging methods (Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures, audio-video models, efficient world models) hands-on, well before they become standard tools.
* **Mentoring:** Supervising undergraduate and graduate researchers and helping them grow into strong ML engineers and scientists.
